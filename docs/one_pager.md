# ALKIMIATECH

**Predictive Neurological Intelligence for Epilepsy Monitoring**

## The Problem

Over 50 million people live with epilepsy worldwide. Current monitoring systems are reactive—they detect seizures after onset. Patients, families, and physicians lack reliable tools to forecast seizure risk in real time, leading to severe anxiety, hospitalizations, and preventable deaths (SUDEP).

## Our Solution

AlkimiaTech is a HealthTech/MedTech research prototype that transforms wearable data into real-time seizure-risk forecasting. We do not replace existing hardware. We function as an **interoperable predictive intelligence layer** that integrates with wearable ecosystems and neurodiagnostic platforms.

## The Technology (3 Layers)

1. **Data Acquisition:** Real-time physiological biomarkers (HR, HRV, SpO₂, movement) via Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) from commercial smartwatches.
2. **Detection & Emergency:** Automated alerts to caregivers and GPS localization during a crisis.
3. **Clinical Platform (Neuromap 3D):** A WebGL-based 3D brain visualization tool that maps physiological activity and clinical EEG data onto anatomical brain regions for intuitive neurological analysis.

## Current Status (MVP)

- ✅ **Functional Prototype:** Flutter app with real Bluetooth integration (tested with Amazfit Bip 6).
- ✅ **Secure Backend:** FastAPI + SQLite with data validation.
- ✅ **Neuromap 3D:** Interactive brain mapping with clinical EEG integration.
- ✅ **Security Tested:** Validated via Kali Linux penetration testing.
